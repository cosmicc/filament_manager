FILAMENT MANAGER - CURA MATERIAL PRINT SETTINGS
================================================

Purpose
-------
This is the complete current list of Cura print settings that Filament Manager
tracks per material and expects to be exposed by the Cura Material Settings
plugin (sometimes called the Extra Print Settings for Materials plugin).

In Cura, open Preferences > Materials > Print Settings, choose "Select
settings", and expose every setting listed below. The Cura setting key is the
authoritative identifier; the display label is included to make each setting
easy to find. There are 54 selected settings in this revision.

Filament Manager's centralized catalog is the source of truth:
src/filament_manager/domain/cura_material_settings.py

When this list changes, update the centralized catalog and this file together.
An automated test rejects any missing, extra, renamed, or mislabeled entry.

Format
------
Cura setting key | Cura display label | value type/unit | note


TEMPERATURE
-----------
material_print_temperature | Printing Temperature | number, C | primary nozzle temperature
material_bed_temperature | Build Plate Temperature | number, C | primary build-plate temperature
default_material_print_temperature | Default Printing Temperature | number, C | synchronized from the primary nozzle temperature
material_print_temperature_layer_0 | Initial Printing Temperature | number, C | independently tracked material setting
material_standby_temperature | Standby Temperature | number, C | independently tracked material setting
material_initial_print_temperature | Initial Printing Temperature | number, C | independently tracked material setting
build_volume_temperature | Build Volume Temperature | number, C | chamber temperature
material_final_print_temperature | Final Printing Temperature | number, C | independently tracked material setting
default_material_bed_temperature | Default Build Plate Temperature | number, C | synchronized from the primary build-plate temperature
material_bed_temperature_layer_0 | Initial Build Plate Temperature | number, C | independently tracked material setting


FLOW
----
material_flow | Flow | number, percent | primary material flow
infill_material_flow | Infill Flow | number, percent | independently tracked material setting
support_material_flow | Support Flow | number, percent | independently tracked material setting
material_flow_layer_0 | Initial Layer Flow | number, percent | independently tracked material setting
roofing_material_flow | Top Surface Skin Flow | number, percent | independently tracked material setting
skirt_brim_material_flow | Skirt/Brim Flow | number, percent | independently tracked material setting


SPEED
-----
speed_layer_0 | Initial Layer Speed | number, mm/s | synchronized from the primary initial-layer speed
speed_wall_x | Inner Wall Speed | number, mm/s | primary inner-wall speed
speed_infill | Infill Speed | number, mm/s | primary infill speed
speed_roofing | Top Surface Skin Speed | number, mm/s | independently tracked material setting
speed_wall_0 | Outer Wall Speed | number, mm/s | primary outer-wall speed
speed_topbottom | Top/Bottom Speed | number, mm/s | primary top/bottom speed
speed_travel_layer_0 | Initial Layer Travel Speed | number, mm/s | independently tracked material setting
speed_print | Print Speed | number, mm/s | primary print speed
speed_travel | Travel Speed | number, mm/s | primary travel speed
speed_print_layer_0 | Initial Layer Print Speed | number, mm/s | synchronized from the primary initial-layer speed
skirt_brim_speed | Skirt/Brim Speed | number, mm/s | independently tracked material setting
speed_support | Support Speed | number, mm/s | primary support speed
speed_wall | Wall Speed | number, mm/s | independently tracked material setting


RETRACTION
----------
retraction_enable | Enable Retraction | boolean | independently tracked material setting
retraction_retract_speed | Retraction Retract Speed | number, mm/s | primary retraction retract speed
retraction_amount | Retraction Distance | number, mm | primary retraction distance
retraction_prime_speed | Retraction Prime Speed | number, mm/s | primary retraction prime speed
limit_support_retractions | Limit Support Retractions | boolean | independently tracked material setting
retraction_min_travel | Retraction Minimum Travel | number, mm | independently tracked material setting
retract_at_layer_change | Retract at Layer Change | boolean | independently tracked material setting


COOLING
-------
cool_fan_speed_min | Regular Fan Speed | number, percent | primary minimum fan speed
cool_fan_enabled | Enable Print Cooling | boolean | primary cooling control
cool_fan_speed | Fan Speed | number, percent | synchronized from the primary maximum fan speed
cool_fan_speed_max | Maximum Fan Speed | number, percent | synchronized alias of the primary maximum fan speed
cool_fan_full_layer | Regular Fan Speed at Layer | number | independently tracked material setting
cool_min_layer_time | Minimum Layer Time | number, s | independently tracked material setting
cool_min_layer_time_fan_speed_max | Minimum Layer Time at Maximum Fan | number, s | independently tracked material setting
cool_min_speed | Minimum Speed | number, mm/s | independently tracked cooling setting


SUPPORT
-------
support_angle | Support Overhang Angle | number, degrees | primary support overhang angle


DIMENSIONAL COMPENSATION
------------------------
xy_offset_layer_0 | Initial Layer Horizontal Expansion | number, mm | independently tracked material setting
hole_xy_offset_max_diameter | Hole Horizontal Expansion Max Diameter | number, mm | independently tracked material setting
xy_offset | Horizontal Expansion | number, mm | independently tracked material setting
hole_xy_offset | Hole Horizontal Expansion | number, mm | independently tracked material setting


KLIPPER SETTINGS PLUGIN
-----------------------
klipper_pressure_advance_factor | Klipper Pressure Advance | number, s | primary pressure-advance value
klipper_smooth_time_enable | Enable Klipper Smooth Time | boolean | independently tracked material setting
klipper_smooth_time_factor | Klipper Smooth Time | number, s | independently tracked material setting


NOT SELECTED THROUGH THIS PLUGIN
--------------------------------
Material Brand and Material Type are managed as Cura material metadata, so they
are intentionally not part of this 54-setting selection list. Filament color,
diameter, density, identity, and preferred build-plate side are also managed by
Filament Manager outside this plugin setting selector.
